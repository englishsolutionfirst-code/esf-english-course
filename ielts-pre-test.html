Part 2 — Reading

Read the passage and answer Questions 9–18.

The Changing Way People Learn English

English has become an important international language for education, employment and communication. In the past, many learners depended primarily on textbooks and classroom instruction. Today, however, students have access to a much wider range of learning resources.

One major development is the increasing use of online learning platforms. Students can attend classes from home, watch recorded lessons and practise their skills using digital materials. This flexibility is particularly useful for people who have busy schedules or live far from language schools.

Technology has also changed the way learners practise English. Mobile applications allow students to review vocabulary, complete grammar exercises and listen to English recordings whenever they have free time. Some applications even provide immediate feedback, which can help learners identify their weaknesses.

Nevertheless, technology does not automatically guarantee successful learning. Students still need motivation, regular practice and appropriate guidance. For example, a learner may download several language applications but rarely use them consistently. Similarly, watching English videos can be useful, but passive viewing may not be enough to develop strong speaking or writing skills.

For this reason, many teachers believe that the most effective approach is to combine technology with structured learning. Online resources can provide flexibility and additional practice, while teachers can provide feedback, explain difficult concepts and help students develop appropriate strategies.

Ultimately, successful English learning depends less on the number of resources available and more on how effectively learners use them. A simple but consistent study routine may be more beneficial than having access to a large number of materials without a clear learning plan.
